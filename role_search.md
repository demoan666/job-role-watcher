## Legend
- **Apply Now** — confirmed live role at time of finding, matches profile.
- **Adjacent / Strong Fit** — real in-house creative capability, role found is close but not exact match, or company is a strong culture/pay fit even if current role is slightly off-profile.
- **Monitor** — strong-fit company (named studio or clear in-house creative capability), no live matching role at time of finding.
- **De-prioritize** — company outsources flagship creative to an agency of record; weak in-house target despite size/fame.
