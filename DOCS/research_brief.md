## What kind of role this is — and is NOT
**Wants:** An **in-house** role — the candidate works directly for a company whose core business is *not* creative/marketing services, but which runs its own **self-sufficient internal creative/motion/video team** producing branding and promotional material for its own social/online presence. The company is the client, not the vendor.

**Explicitly excludes:**
- Agency jobs (creative agencies, ad agencies, production companies serving external clients).
- Outsourced/staffing placements where a staffing firm (e.g. Williams Lea) places the candidate *inside* a client's in-house studio but employs them — borderline; flag these clearly as "staffing placement" rather than true in-house employment, don't silently merge them into direct in-house findings.
- Companies whose flagship creative work is entirely outsourced to an external agency of record, even if they have some internal design function (e.g. companies where the AOR does all brand film/campaign work and internal staff only do product/UX design).
- **Cameraman / cinematographer roles — exclude these entirely, do not surface them even as adjacent/monitor.**

**High-signal heuristic:** Companies with a **named internal creative studio or in-house agency** (e.g. Ericsson's "M&C Hub," Experian's "In-House Creative Studio," Swisscom's "B2B Creation Factory," Wise's in-house brand/creative team) are the strongest targets — a name signals real headcount, a career path, and a quality bar. Actively hunt for these names, not just job titles.
