# Hidden Dream-Job Map: In-House Creative/Motion Roles at B2B-Dominant Corporations (2026)

## TL;DR
- **Apply now:** Five confirmed live in-house motion/video roles fit the candidate almost perfectly — **Experian's In-House Creative Studio** (multiple Motion Designer / Motion Designer & Video Editor / Senior Motion Designer roles, London & Coventry, hybrid), **Storyblok's fully-remote "Motion & Video Designer – Europe"** (best fit for a Poland-based EU candidate), **Wise Business Motion Designer** (London, 30+ person in-house brand team), **Swisscom's "Video & Motion Designer 80–100%"** for its in-house "B2B Creation Factory" (Switzerland, hybrid), and **Ericsson's M&C Hub AI Motion Design roles** (currently posted in Budapest).
- **The pattern that works:** The high-signal targets are companies with a *named* in-house studio — Ericsson **"M&C Hub,"** Experian **"In-House Creative Studio,"** Wise's in-house brand agency, Swisscom **"B2B Creation Factory,"** Maersk/APM Terminals' in-house video studio in The Hague, and SAP's internal creative community. These indicate real headcount, career paths, and quality expectations, and most hire directly through the parent's careers portal.
- **Remote reality for a Poland-based EU candidate:** Truly remote-first EU-hireable roles are rare in this segment; **Storyblok is the standout** (remote from Europe). Most others are hybrid and tied to a specific HQ city (London, Aarhus, Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Bern, Stockholm), so relocation or willingness to travel to a hub is the realistic path for the strongest targets.

## Key Findings
- The candidate's exact niche — large, B2B-dominant, not-consumer-famous companies running self-sufficient in-house creative teams — is real and hiring, but openings are episodic. The single most reliable route is to track named in-house studios rather than generic job boards.
- AI-assisted and template/system-building skills (the candidate's "vibecoding," MOGRTs, scripting strengths) are now explicitly requested in these postings. Ericsson advertises an "AI Motion Design Specialist"; Experian wants "AI native" motion designers who are "Comfortable with automation, adapting, versioning and resizing assets"; Storyblok wants someone to "Lead and evolve Storyblok's video design system … templates … reusable assets" — a near-perfect match for a tool-builder.
- Nordic industrial giants (Vestas, Grundfos, Danfoss, Atlas Copco, Wärtsilä) clearly run in-house brand/motion systems (Atlas Copco even publishes a public "motion branding toolbox"), but they surface dedicated motion/video vacancies only occasionally — these are "monitor" targets.
- Some famous names on the candidate's list actually outsource their flagship creative and are weaker in-house targets than expected: **Bosch** (Droga5 as US AoR), **Siemens AG** (Saatchi & Saatchi global), and **DHL** (Strichpunkt + AI self-service tooling) do not run a dedicated named in-house film/motion studio.

## Caveats
- **Live-status volatility:** Job postings move fast. Several roles cited here were live at search time (mid-2026) but must be re-verified on the official portal before applying; some aggregator listings may be stale.
- **Salary transparency is thin in this segment:** Despite EU pay-transparency trends, most of these specific postings did not disclose ranges. The one UK band seen (~£28k–£33k for a Motion Designer) suggests in-house corporate motion pay is mid-market, not agency-senior-level — negotiate on seniority and tool-building value.
- **"In-house" is not always 100% in-house:** Even companies with real internal studios (SAP, Adyen, Deutsche Telekom, DHL) also use external agencies for flagship brand work. This does not disqualify them, but temper expectations about owning the marquee campaigns.
- **Geography vs. remote tension:** The candidate's remote-first preference conflicts with the reality that the strongest named-studio targets (Experian, Wise, Swisscom, Maersk, Klarna, Adyen) are hub-tied and hybrid. Storyblok is the cleanest resolution of that tension today.
- **Some listings were US-based** (Siemens Healthineers motion roles, plus Salesforce/Slack and Google Brand Studio motion roles that surfaced in searches) and are not EU-hireable without sponsorship; they are included only as evidence the in-house function exists, not as apply-now targets.
