197 countries/territories, paths annotated by `id` with ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
codes (entities without an official code are prefixed with an underscore,
e.g. `_somaliland`). world-map.svg vendored unmodified.

country-codes.json's `continent` field is merged in from a second source:

Source: https://github.com/lukes/ISO-3166-Countries-with-Regional-Codes
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

That dataset's `region` field (UN M49 continental grouping) was matched onto
each entry by ISO alpha-2 code. Two entries have no ISO/UN region assignment
and were set by hand on plain geography, not a political claim: `_somaliland`
(Africa) and `tw`/Taiwan (Asia, unassigned in the UN M49 scheme due to its
disputed status).
